\title{The Opportunity Cost of Carbon Calculation Errors in Green Capital Budgeting}
\authormark{Carbon errors and green budgeting}
\titlemark{Carbon errors and green budgeting}
\keywords{carbon accounting | environmental strategy | green capital budgeting | sustainability assurance | capital allocation | opportunity cost}
\abstract[ABSTRACT]{Errors in carbon intensity (emissions per unit of electricity) can increase total net present value in green capital budgeting while reducing the value of projects meeting the emissions rule. Across 48 constructed energy projects in eight portfolios, two errors understating intensity changed 129 funding decisions; 55 retained every originally funded qualifying project. We separate losses through exclusion and capital displacement. For projects funded in full or not at all, with positive additive costs, additive values and one budget allowing unspent funds, displacement occurs exactly when ineligible spending exceeds the capital left after reserving the least expensive value-maximizing portfolio of projects eligible under both calculations. At three error scales, multiplying rather than dividing intensity by the same factor caused greater average loss for every combination of financial reassignments within portfolios, weighting portfolios, budgets and ceilings equally. The ranking could reverse at 45\% of total project costs.}
