# Two public project records

## Vasilikos Unit 6, Cyprus

The European Investment Bank published a project appraisal with a carbon-intensity comparison and subsequently signed a loan. The project scoreboard dated 16 December 2020 gives approximate design estimates of 380 g CO₂/kWh on natural gas and 510 on backup distillate. The bank compares its gas estimate with the earlier 550 and newer 250 g CO₂/kWh standards. From the displayed approximations the two fuels are respectively about 170 and 40 below the earlier standard and 130 and 260 above the newer one. These are engineering estimates for envisaged fuel modes. The technology supplier had yet to be chosen. [EIB project scoreboard, p. 1](https://www.eib.org/attachments/registers/137305639.pdf)

The new policy was adopted on 14 November 2019 with a transition to the end of 2021 for projects already under appraisal. The bank subsequently listed this project among its grandfathered approvals and recorded PIN approval in October 2019. The Board approved an investment loan up to €76 million on 16 December 2020. The project register records a €76 million signature on 17 December. These events distinguish entry into appraisal, Board approval and signature. The register gives an approximate project cost of €152 million. [2019 policy, pp. 4 and 15](https://www.eib.org/files/publications/strategies/eib_energy_lending_policy_en.pdf) · [Mid-term review, Annex A, p. 26](https://www.eib.org/attachments/lucalli/20230012_mid-term_review_of_the_energy_lending_policy_en.pdf) · [Board minutes, item 24, PDF p. 9](https://www.eib.org/files/documents/governing-bodies/ca_minutes_20201216.pdf) · [Project register, Signature(s)](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20190376)

The 2013 criteria required an economic assessment alongside a strict below-EPS test and provided for limited exceptions on isolated systems. Both published fuel estimates fell below 550. [2013 screening criteria, p. 26, paragraphs 113–115](https://www.eib.org/files/publications/strategies/eib_energy_lending_criteria_en.pdf)

The bank's EPS concerns plant CO₂ per generated kWh. A change from grams/kWh to kilograms/MWh leaves the numerical value unchanged for the same substance and metering basis. The public scoreboard supplies estimates without the underlying emissions masses, electricity quantities, purchased-power treatment or gross/net metering convention. The bank's environmental data sheet separately estimates annual absolute emissions and emissions savings against a counterfactual. Those figures carry a different boundary and unit. The published record supports a historical financing appraisal and signature. It does not identify a calculation defect, isolate the financing effect of the threshold, or permit classification under the study's net-output, Scope 1 + 2 rule. [EPS methodology, p. 1](https://www.eib.org/files/consultations/elp_methodology_emission_performance_standard_20130722_en.pdf) · [Environmental and Social Data Sheet, p. 2](https://www.eib.org/attachments/registers/130185269.pdf)

## Copiulemu landfill gas, Chile

The SGS verification report for CDM project 0096 documents a wrong-column formula. The December 2006 project methane calculation referenced O194:O224, the regulatory-deduction column, in place of N194:N224. The two entries initially both read 407.92 tCO₂e. In the final monthly table, methane destroyed under the project was 2,329.57 tCO₂e, the regulatory deduction was 396.03, and electricity use contributed 1.97. Subtraction produces 1,931.57, which rounds to the verified 1,932. The eight-month calculation from the printed total components yields 19,565.56 − 3,326.15 − 24.68 = 16,214.73, which rounds to 16,215 tCO₂e. All eight rounded monthly entries reproduce individually. [SGS verification report, pp. 9–11 and 14](https://cdm.unfccc.int/Projects/DB/DNV-CUK1126875537.72/iProcess/SGS-UKL1188294574.74/ReviewInitialComments/NEXNYPXPNJFC9OOG8BF6DBO3EOC9Q4)

The same table lists 16,371 tCO₂e originally reported, 16,403 in its second version and 16,215 in its third. The verifier also revised flare efficiency and addressed methane measurement. The three totals do not isolate a causal contribution for the wrong column. The project registry records a request for 16,403 CERs for 1 December 2006–31 July 2007 and lists the request as rejected. The CDM Board attributed rejection to the evidence on methane sampling time, sample size and the required 95% confidence level. The difference of 188 tCO₂e between verification versions therefore cannot be counted as credits lost through that formula. [Project register, issuance periods](https://cdm.unfccc.int/Projects/DB/DNV-CUK1126875537.72/view) · [Executive Board meeting 43, p. 17, para. 54](https://cdm.unfccc.int/EB/043/eb43rep.pdf)

## Reproduce the arithmetic

Run `python3 replay.py` from this folder. The script uses the transcribed primary-source values in `observations.csv` and `copiulemu_months.csv` and prints the values in `verified_quantities.json`. The source register identifies each document, version, page and durable primary identifier. Hashes cover original bytes when available. The SGS report and UNFCCC project register were checked in the first-party full-text rendering, which did not provide original bytes for hashing. The registry and Board separately document the decision.
